Meet Your Teacher

Rommel

Drums

Get ready to unleash your inner beat with Rommel, the drum teacher who knows how to make learning fun and exciting. With years of experience as a performer and educator, Rommel brings a wealth of knowledge to the table.

Having graduated with a Bachelor of Music in Percussion, Rommel honed his skills under the guidance of renowned drummer Michael Alba. He has graced the stage as part of the Shoreline Band and collaborated with popular artists like Jireh Lim, rocking music tours and festivals across the Philippines.

Rommel understands that each student is unique, and he caters to their individual needs and learning styles. He believes that everyone has a talent for music, and his goal is to help his students transform their potential into practical drumming skills.

Whether you are aiming for grade exams or simply looking for a stress-relieving hobby, Rommel has you covered. He has a track record of success coaching students through their exams, but he is equally passionate about making drumming a fun and enjoyable experience.

Meet Your Teacher

Jen

Voice

With over 15 years of teaching experience, Jen believes that every student is unique and deserves an approach that is tailored to their individual needs, strengths, and learning style. Her teaching is flexible and student-centred, with lessons specially designed to help each student grow at their own pace.

She believes that music education goes beyond developing technical skills. She strives to nurture her students emotionally, mentally, and musically, creating a safe and encouraging environment where they can feel confident, express themselves, and enjoy learning. Her lessons strike a balance between fun and structured learning, ensuring that students develop proper vocal technique while maintaining their enthusiasm and love for music.

She graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Music Education, majoring in Voice and minoring in Piano, from the University of Santo Tomas, Philippines. She also holds a Professional Certificate from Berklee College of Music, USA, and has completed Levels 1 and 2 of Estill Voice Training (EVT) at the Royal Academy of Music in London.

As an educator, she believes that learning never ends. Her goal is not only to teach students how to sing, but to inspire a lifelong appreciation and love for music—encouraging them to remain curious, keep learning, and continue growing as musicians.

“Every student has their own strengths, personality, and way of learning. My goal is to meet them where they are, nurture their potential, and help them discover the joy of music while building strong and proper technique.”

Meet Your Teacher

Cindy

Piano

Cindy is an award-winning educator, top Spotify artist (100,000+ streams across 70+ countries), and adjudicator for international piano competitions. With over 15 years of teaching experience, her students include members of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts’ prestigious junior music programme — a selective pathway for young talents. Many have won prizes in international piano competitions and earned admission to top universities worldwide. Beyond the studio, students benefit from frequent live performance opportunities, with selected individuals having their recordings featured on Spotify and other streaming platforms, as well as engaging with world-class musicians.

Beyond teaching, Cindy has built a distinctive career as a global streaming artist. Her entirely self-composed, arranged, and performed atmospheric pieces offer a genuine, heartfelt connection through deeply personal narratives. As an artist, she helps students find their own musical voice, not just repeat what's on the page.

Meet Your Teacher

JT

Guitar & Woodwinds

Get ready to make music your superpower with JT, the guitar and woodwinds teacher who knows how to bring energy and creativity to every lesson. With years of experience as a versatile performer and educator, JT brings a wealth of knowledge to the table.

Having studied music at St. Paul University Manila, JT has built a dynamic career both on and off the stage. He has graced the stage with renowned ensembles like the Manila Philharmonic Orchestra and the Metro Manila Concert Orchestra, and accompanied celebrated vocalists such as Lani Misalucha and Jed Madela in everything from lively community events to grand orchestral performances.

As a dedicated educator, JT currently serves as the Music Director of The Feast Hong Kong and a music teacher at Silvermine Bay School. He understands that each student is unique, and he caters to their individual needs to nurture their musical talent. His goal is to help his students transform their potential and creativity into practical, confident playing.

Whether you are strumming your very first chord, mastering complex woodwind techniques, or simply looking for an engaging new outlet, JT has you covered. He has the skills and experience to guide you in mastering your instrument, but he is equally passionate about making sure you play with confidence and have fun along the way.
